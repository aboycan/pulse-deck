## Free of charge, and open source

PulseDeck is **free software**. You can download, use, copy, modify and share
it at no cost, for any purpose. There are no ads, no paid tiers, no accounts,
no telemetry and no tracking. PulseDeck doesn't send any data anywhere. The
only network access happens when *you* run setup, which downloads the
components listed below.

"Freeware" here means free of charge. PulseDeck is also *open source*: the
complete source code is public, and you're free to study and change it.

endorsed by, or derived from Winamp or its owners. It contains no Winamp

## Artwork and metadata

PulseDeck reads album artwork only from your own files: pictures embedded
in your audio files, or image files in the same folder. It never downloads
artwork or metadata from the internet.
